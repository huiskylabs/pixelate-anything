# Gallery sources and licenses

The generated images, palettes and music are still in `examples/gallery` (the demo scripts write there; moving them is a later step). They are regenerated by:
- `demo/cli_demo.sh`: a palette, scene exports and era checks through the CLI.
- `demo/mcp_demo.py`: the rebuilds, retro screens and music, made through the MCP server.

## References that are not redistributed here

These are rebuilt rather than converted. The photos themselves are not in the repository.

## Original work

`library/retro-screens` (Rooftop Run, Island Village, Pixel Harbor) and `library/lantern-isle` are
original in their characters, places, titles and text, except that the Pixel Harbor city behind the title
screen is the `library/san-francisco` rebuild, with the photo caveat above. Console names in the era presets describe
hardware limits only.
