# Gallery sources and licenses

Everything in `examples/gallery` is regenerated by:
- `demo/cli_demo.sh`: a palette, scene exports and era checks through the CLI.
- `demo/mcp_demo.py`: the rebuilds, retro screens and music, made through the MCP server.

## References that are not redistributed here

These are rebuilt rather than converted. The photos themselves are not in the repository.

| rebuild | reference | where |
|---|---|---|
| `examples/san-francisco` | aerial photo of the Financial District and the Transamerica Pyramid | supplied by the user |
| `examples/santorini` | "Houses on Hill" by Vasilis Karkalas | https://www.pexels.com/photo/houses-on-hill-11222833/ (Pexels license) |

## Original work

`examples/retro` (Rooftop Run, Island Village, Pixel Harbor) and `examples/dragon` (Dragonfire Keep)
are all original: characters, places, titles and text. Console names in the era presets describe
hardware limits only.
