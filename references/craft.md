# Craft: making it look hand-made

This page is an index. The craft rules now live in three files, one per stage of a job:

- [brief.md](brief.md): read the intent, route as a hint, write the brief (the 11 sections, or a
  short brief for a small ask), self-review with `--lint`, get the user's OK. "Write the brief
  first" and "From one line to a brief" are here.
- [drawing.md](drawing.md): how to draw: light, palette, gradients, objects, characters, phases,
  motion, composition.
- [delivery.md](delivery.md): the gate (`pixelate check FRAME.png --style S --scale N`), the still
  for go/no-go, the animation plan, export, and the failure checklist.
