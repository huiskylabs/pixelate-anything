# A piece's history

A piece may keep work and evidence under `library/<id>/history/`. `pixelate library add` copies the source's history and writes `history/add-report.json`: the two clean renders' output hashes, image gate results, and the fact that grade, approval and redistribution rights were **not established**. That record does not mean the run's art was accepted. Review records and `DELIVERY.md` bind different facts to exact frame bytes; keep them separate.

For an independently reviewed draft, keep the actual 1× draft frame, its brief and the external lead's saved report, the requested fixes, and the next frame that incorporated them. Record the frame's SHA-256 and the reviewer/round in a dated Markdown trail. A self-critique, a gate failure, a judge box and a golden mismatch are not interchangeable with an independent grade. Preserve the default three graded lead rounds per full author stage and identify the one lead; specialists only observe. An unreviewed remix stays **NOT REVIEWED**. Never edit a saved review's grade to fit a later revision.

A **curated reject** needs a genuine rejected frame, a documented reason from a human or independent reviewer, the exact frame and brief hashes, and permission to keep and redistribute every source (including reference photos, sprites and audio). Label it *rejected* in its trail; do not let search/gallery treat it as a good piece. Do not manufacture rejects from synthetic detector failures or declare a study's technical-gate failure an art-quality rejection. No labelled reject corpus or calibrated good/bad margin has been established yet; keep the detectors as warnings until such evidence exists. Training/export in phase 6 additionally needs the held-out evaluation and rights review. A copied `history/` directory or an `add-report.json` is not a preference dataset.
