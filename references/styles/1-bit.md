# 1-bit

## Essence

Two colours, black and one paper white. Fixed dither patterns stand in for grey, each with one job. Shapes read as silhouettes first, and light is white left in the black.

## What it is not

- Not `gameboy` (`--era gameboy`): that has four shades; this has two.
- Not `contre-jour`: no light layer and no soft fog; mist is a 25% pattern.
- Not a dithered photo: patterns are placed by role, never an error-diffusion gradient.

## Pitfalls

- A third colour sneaking in through scaling or a blend: the gate counts colours and allows two.
- Pattern soup: when every surface has its own dither the frame turns to noise; keep five patterns at most, each with one role.
- Patterns that move with the camera: a pattern stays fixed to the screen grid or to its object, never shimmers.
- Lost silhouettes: test each key object as a solid black shape first.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, weather, scale and camera.
- Black-heavy or white-heavy; paper tone (warm cream, cool blue-white, pale green).
- Which patterns, and which role each one has.
- Music: one or two cold voices, tempo free.
