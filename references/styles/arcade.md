# arcade

## Essence

Loud action at Neo Geo density: warm hand-modelled actors on a cold dark world, effects as opaque sprites, every frame busy and readable.

## What it is not

- Not `illustrated`: actors, not monuments, fill the frame.
- Not a modern glow style: no soft light layer of any kind, explosions are opaque pixels.
- Not borrowed IP: soldiers, machines and bosses are originals.

## Pitfalls

- Actors that sink into the world: the warm/cold split must hold.
- Effects drawn as gradients: fire, smoke and sparks are opaque clusters with stepped frames.
- Too clean: arcade density means debris, shells, dust and secondary action.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, faction, vehicles, the boss, the setting.
- Palette pair (any warm actors on any cold world), how busy the frame is, the action beat.
- Music: driving chip or FM-like, tempo free.
