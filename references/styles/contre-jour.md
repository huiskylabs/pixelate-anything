# contre-jour

## Essence

Night seen against the light. Flat dark silhouettes stand in front of glowing air; every soft edge lives in one screen-resolution light layer between depth planes, never in the pixels. The mood is mysterious and still: a ritual, a forest clearing, a pillar of light, figures tiny in a big dark set.

## What it is not

- Not `cosy-lightmap`: no warm interior lamp map; the light is behind the subject, not around it.
- Not `neon-noir`: no exact-black void and no coloured wall-wash; the dark is fog and air, not a slab in space.
- Not a film still: no perspective camera, no lens flare, no grain.

## Pitfalls

- Lighting the near plane: it must stay a flat dark cut-out or the depth collapses.
- Fog everywhere: keep at least one dark mass that the glow does not reach, or the frame turns to grey soup.
- Hero lost in the haze: the hero's silhouette needs the frame's strongest value break.
- Secondary objects drowned: a shrine, a lantern, a second figure still need their own readable silhouette keys.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, setting, time of night, weather, season.
- Light colour and where the source sits, as long as it is behind the subject.
- Number and spacing of depth planes (three or more), camera height, the story beat of the loop.
- Music instruments and tempo within a slow, spacious feel.
