# cosy-lightmap

## Essence

A warm, cosy 16-bit place at night or underground: a farm, a cave, a cabin interior. The pixel layer sits under one smooth multiplied lamp map at screen resolution; actors move in screen pixels.

## What it is not

- Not `contre-jour`: the light surrounds the subject from lamps, it is not behind it.
- Not `neon-noir`: warm, not cold; no exact-black void.
- Not a lit filter: the lamp map is computed from the scene's own lamps and masks.

## Pitfalls

- A lifted void: the darkness beyond the lamps stays dark.
- Lamps without occluders: furniture and walls must cut the light.
- Screen-blend light: this style multiplies, never screens.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, the place, the lamps, the season outside.
- Who lives there and what they do, secondary objects.
- Music: gentle and warm, instruments free.
