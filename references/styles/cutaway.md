# cutaway

## Essence

A building, ship or station sliced open on a strict military projection: one flat cut face frames lit rooms, and tiny people do one readable job in each. The viewer's pleasure is reading room after room.

## What it is not

- Not `sticker-iso`: 2:1 iso streets are viewed whole from outside; here the walls are cut and the inside is the subject.
- Not `tactics`: no floating board, no unit grid, rooms are full and lit.
- Not true 30-degree iso or perspective: military projection only (plan at true shape turned 45 degrees, verticals 1:1).

## Pitfalls

- Empty rooms: every room needs a task and a figure doing it.
- A textured cut face: the cut is one flat colour with a hard edge, or the rooms lose their frame.
- Same-size rooms in a grid: vary room sizes and link them with ladders, pipes and cables.
- Projection drift: one wrong angle breaks the whole section; draw with `iso` military mode.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- What is cut (a ship, a tree house, a mine, a station) and where it sits (sea, sky, ground).
- The cut-face colour, number and shape of rooms, the outside environment.
- Music: busy but calm, instruments free.
