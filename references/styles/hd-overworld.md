# hd-overworld

## Essence

A bright adventure world seen from 3/4 above: coasts, villages, cliffs, day into night. Long low-key ramps whose chroma rises toward the light, translucent clouds and water, light snapped to the art grid.

## What it is not

- Not `cosy-lightmap`: no single smooth lamp map at screen resolution.
- Not `tactics`: no floating diorama in the dark, the world runs to the frame edges.
- Not a 3D render: no perspective, no screen light shafts or lens flares.

## Pitfalls

- Short ramps that flatten the terrain: use the long ramps the preset gives.
- Filters for time of day: day into night is a palette and light-map change, never a tint.
- A map with no life: villages need figures, boats, smoke and small stories.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, region, time of day, season, weather.
- Camera crop and framing within 3/4 top-down, the hero and the secondary objects.
- Music: adventurous or calm, instruments free.
