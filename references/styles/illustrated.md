# illustrated

## Essence

Illustration-grade sky worlds: floating islands, carved stone, a monument filling the frame, depth by palette haze, a frame drawn into the art.

## What it is not

- Not `pastel-cell`: mid-key carved form, not a high-key wash.
- Not a painting filter: every stroke is pixel clusters, depth is palette haze, never blur.
- Not dark: no low-key scenery.

## Pitfalls

- A monument too small: it should fill the frame.
- Soft light layers: depth comes from palette haze only.
- Stone without carving: the material needs readable cut detail at 1x.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, the monument, the sky world, the time of day.
- The drawn frame's motif, the haze colour, secondary objects such as airships and birds.
- Music: grand or gentle, instruments free.
