# lofi

## Essence

The toolkit's original look: a calm looping cover for music, light drawn with palette colours only, Bayer gradients, own-colour outlines.

## What it is not

- Not `pastel-cell`: palette-step light, not native bloom.
- Not `cosy-lightmap`: no light layer at all.
- Not a stream-thumbnail template: the scene is drawn new each time.

## Pitfalls

- Glow, bloom or soft shadows: all banned in this style.
- Narrow gradient bands that read as stripes.
- A pretty but empty scene: lofi still needs a story beat and lived-in detail.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, setting, season, time of day, weather.
- The hero, the story beat of the loop, secondary objects.
- Music: lofi is the default, but any calm style fits.
