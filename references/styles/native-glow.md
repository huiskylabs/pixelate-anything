# native-glow

## Essence

Low-key ruins or tech temples on a vertical spine of light. Magenta and cyan glow, runes and sigils; the light is continuous but snapped to the art grid, falling off exponentially from its sources.

## What it is not

- Not `contre-jour`: the softness never goes below the art pixel.
- Not `neon-noir`: no exact-black void and no city or bar; the world is ancient and continuous.
- Not a sci-fi HUD: glyphs belong to the stone, never overlaid on the screen.

## Pitfalls

- Glow spread too wide: keep most of the frame low-key so the spine reads.
- Every rune lit equally: one source dominates, the rest are rationed.
- Screen-resolution blur sneaking in through a soft post op.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, architecture, the two glow hues (any complementary pair), figures and their story.
- Where the spine of light falls, vertical or tilted.
- Music: low drones and sparse bells are the default, the instruments are free.
