# neon-noir

## Essence

A neon-lit night interior or city: one lit room or street slab floating in exact black, coloured wall-wash light multiplied per art pixel, rain on neon.

## What it is not

- Not `contre-jour`: the dark is exact black, not glowing fog.
- Not `cosy-lightmap`: the light is cold and coloured, not warm and smooth.
- Not cyberpunk IP: no borrowed signage, logos or brands; signs are original glyphs.

## Pitfalls

- Lifting the black: the void must stay exact black.
- Hairline rain: rain is drawn in strokes that survive the scale.
- Too many colours of neon: ration the accent, one or two hues.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, the room or street, the story beat, the characters.
- The neon hues, weather, the signage text.
- Music: noir or synth, tempo free.
