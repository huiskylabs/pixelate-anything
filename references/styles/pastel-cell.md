# pastel-cell

## Essence

A high-key, one-hue mood: pink, violet or dusk light over a big vertical world, a tiny expressive hero, weather moving through. Sky in undithered strata, clouds as flat masses, soft glow computed per art pixel so every cell stays one flat colour.

## What it is not

- Not `illustrated`: no carved mid-key stone or painted vista; this world is light and airy, values stay high.
- Not `lofi`: no Bayer gradients and no darker palette-step night.
- Not a pastel filter on a normal scene: the hue window is designed, not tinted on top.

## Pitfalls

- Dark scenery creeping in: the median value must stay high or the style is gone.
- Hairline rain or one-pixel weather that vanishes at scale: weather is drawn as masses and strokes of 2 px or more.
- A hero too small to read its pose: tiny, but with a clear silhouette and one accent above the paints' chroma.
- Empty sky: strata, cloud masses and secondary objects (kites, birds, flags) carry the density.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, the one hue (any hue window), weather, time of day as long as it stays high-key.
- Hero, secondary objects, story beat, wind direction.
- Vertical or horizontal canvas within the preset size, music feel and instruments.
