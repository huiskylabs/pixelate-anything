# sticker-iso

## Essence

A dense, sunny isometric town where the crowd and the little stories are the subject. Gamut-edge paint, black silhouette outlines, no light and no shadow, full bleed.

## What it is not

- Not `tactics`: no dark void, the town fills the frame edge to edge.
- Not a lit scene: no light, no cast shadow, no time-of-day grade.
- Not a map: it is about the people and their stories, not the layout.

## Pitfalls

- Empty areas: every block needs people, objects and a small story.
- Repeated stamps: the same figure or prop copied reads as fake; vary pose, colour and action.
- Muddy paint: colours sit at the gamut edge, never mixed toward grey.
- A frame of paint everywhere fails the budget. At most 55% of it may be chromatic, so 45% or more is
  black, white or dark neutral; at least 30% sits at L .3 or below and at least 15% above L .7. Plan it
  before drawing: a dark ground (asphalt, a navy street, dark roofs) and the black outlines carry the
  dark share, white walls carry the light share, and paint is the accent on shutters, awnings, clothes
  and the hero.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, city, era of the buildings, the season of the clothes.
- Which little stories, how dense, which landmark anchors the frame.
- Music: bright and busy, instruments free.
