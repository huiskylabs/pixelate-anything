# tactics

## Essence

A calm, readable strategy board: a 2:1 iso diorama floating in the dark, five greys, colour rationed to a few accents, hard cast shadows, room for UI.

## What it is not

- Not `sticker-iso`: the board floats in dark space and colour is scarce.
- Not `hd-overworld`: no running world to the frame edges, the island is a set piece.
- Not true 30-degree iso or perspective: 2:1 only.

## Pitfalls

- Saturated scenery: only units and objectives carry colour.
- Soft light: shadows are hard and cast, never blurred.
- An unreadable board: tiles, height steps and unit silhouettes must read at 1x.

## Variation space

Free in every piece, so two pieces in this style should not look alike:

- Subject, terrain, the units, the objective, the UI.
- The accent colours (one to three), board shape and size.
- Music: measured, strategic, instruments free.
