# Visual specialist scopes

The lead art director alone grades the full work using `art-director.md`. Specialists inspect the same brief and frame (or full brief and block-in); they give concrete observations, not grades, 12-point scores or final ranked fixes.

- **composition:** focal hierarchy, negative space, silhouette at thumbnail scale, plane shares, crop and camera/perspective fidelity. Identify the affected native region or brief line.
- **lighting:** value grouping, light direction, colour roles, chroma hierarchy, cast shadows and material response. Check suggestions against the named lights and physics.
- **set:** architecture, props, grounding, depth and lived-in story of place. Identify specific repeated, missing or misplaced forms and their location.
- **character:** pose, anatomy, expression, readable silhouette, scale, interaction with the set and story. If no character was promised, report that rather than inventing one.

Report only what you can see or the brief actually specifies, with concrete located suggestions and what already works. Do not edit the work or override the brief's light, wind or physics.
